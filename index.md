Written companion lessons for the Priors Lab computer science videos. Each one has the core idea, a worked example from the video, common mistakes, and three self-check questions with answers.

- **[Rate Limiting Explained: Why "5 per Minute" Let 10 Through](/lessons/rate-limiting/)**: How five rate-limiting algorithms decide which bursts get through, and how to enforce one limit across many servers. [Watch the video](https://youtu.be/qRIEZOW4m-Y)
- **[Why Does a 1% Hit Rate Drop Double Database Load?](/lessons/caching/)**: Why the database only feels the misses, plus eviction, stale data and cache stampedes. [Watch the video](https://youtu.be/_7ZKsem-uzY)
- **[Forty Arrivals, and Thirty of Them Time Out](/lessons/load-balancing/)**: How a load balancer picks a server for each connection, and why the slowest hop sets the wait. [Watch the video](https://youtu.be/65ii01AIsAE)
- **[A Million Visitors, One IP Address: Reverse Proxies Explained](/lessons/reverse-proxies/)**: One front door for your servers, and which forwarded headers you can trust. [Watch the video](https://youtu.be/SOnqWmqaq8A)

*The videos are narrated by an AI-generated (synthetic) voice. The lessons are written from the video scripts. Example numbers marked "illustrative" are worked examples for teaching, not production measurements.*
